okay but what does it do

  • It remembers the last 30 secondsStart rolling before you play. The app keeps a rolling buffer so you can save what just happened instead of recording everything and hoping something cool happens.
  • "meta clip that"Use the voice trigger to save a clip without stopping what you're doing. Perfect for those moments when your hands are busy.
  • Controller buttons work tooPrefer pressing a button? Set up a controller trigger instead. Because sometimes yelling at your headset isn't the move.
  • Your clips have their own galleryFind the moments you've saved, check their length and when you grabbed them, then open them in a video player.
  • Made for actually playing gamesThe goal is to capture your gameplay without making the game feel like a slideshow. Performance can vary depending on your device and settings.

how do i use this thing

  1. Download the APK and install it on your supported device.
  2. Open Meta Clip! and set up your preferred trigger.
  3. Start rolling, then go play whatever game you're into.
  4. Something crazy happens? Trigger the clip and check your gallery.

First time installing an APK? You may need to allow installation from your chosen file manager or browser. Only install APKs you trust.
